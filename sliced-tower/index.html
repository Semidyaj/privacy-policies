Sliced Tower — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This policy describes how the Android game Sliced Tower (package com.semidyaj.slicedtower) handles your information.

Data we collect

Sliced Tower does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal or usage data. The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting, and it works without an internet connection.

Data stored on your device

Your best score and the sound setting are saved only on your device. They never leave it, and they are deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Children

Since the game collects no data at all, it collects no data from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game starts collecting data (for example, to show ads or an online leaderboard), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
